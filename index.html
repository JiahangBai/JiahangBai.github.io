I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Jilin University and an embodied AI algorithm intern at Dexmal.

My research interests lie in embodied AI and robot learning, with a particular focus on memory in vision-language-action models and real-world robotic manipulation. I am interested in how robots retain past observations and use them to make decisions over long-horizon tasks.

Research Interests

ROBOT MEMORY & DECISION MAKING

Memory for Vision-Language-Action Models

I study how historical observations can support robotic manipulation when the current image alone is insufficient. My ongoing work explores memory representations, key-event selection, and the use of memory in action prediction.

REAL-WORLD ROBOT LEARNING

Learning Manipulation from Demonstrations

My practical work spans demonstration collection, data processing, policy training, evaluation, and deployment. On the Baxter platform, I work on garment-folding tasks and study failures arising from occlusion, deformable objects, and task-stage transitions.
