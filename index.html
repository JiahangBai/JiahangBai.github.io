Research Interests

Context scaling and in-context learning

S²Mem asked what a robot should remember. Now I’m interested in what a policy can learn from a failed grasp. As more context becomes available, can it use its last few attempts to adjust its next action at test time?

Action representation

Two grasps can look alike even when one holds and the other slips. I’d like to train an action tokenizer on dexterous hand interactions and study which differences it encodes, and whether those tokens help a vision-language model predict the outcome of contact.

Selected Papers

S²Mem: From Sparse Cues to Decision-Relevant Memory in Vision-Language-Action Models

Jiahang Bai, Leyang Wang, Zixuan Huang, Wenxuan Guo, Xutao Sun, Kaixi Ying, Xiaowen Qin, Ze Chen, Erjin Zhou, Yixing Gao

Jilin University · Dexmal · Tsinghua University

Under review at CVPR 2027 · Sole first author

Long-horizon manipulation often depends on fleeting visual cues, and most demonstrated actions offer little supervision for using history. As frames leave a short-term window, S²Mem uses subsequent robot motion to decide which to keep in long-term memory. An event-aligned prediction objective trains memory queries to recover frozen DINOv2 features of masked events. During action training, LoRA adapts the language model while the pretrained VLM backbone stays frozen.
